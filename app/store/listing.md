**Description** (4000 max):

The Way is a Bible study you play. You are not yet convinced. Your teacher Nicodemus sends you north to watch the man from Nazareth, and to write down what you see.

Walk through the Gospels and the early church as an eyewitness: from the night of his birth, through Galilee, the road to Jerusalem, the cross and the empty tomb, to Pentecost, Paul's journeys, the fall of the Temple and John's vision on Patmos. Over fifty episodes, and every word of Scripture in them is quoted exactly.

PLAY AS A WITNESS
• Choose your narrator: Eleazar, a young scribe trained in the Law, or Elisheva, Nicodemus's niece. Or play both accounts.
• Walk the streets of Capernaum, stand in the crowd, talk with the people who were there, and note what you see.
• Live remembered scenes: Mary on the night of the birth, the shepherds' field where you sweep your finger across the angels and they sing, the boy Jesus among the teachers.
• At the end of each episode, write your report and decide what you believe about what you saw.

STUDY AS YOU GO
• A study page for every episode: the full passage, parallel accounts, Greek word studies, history and the places on the map.
• A verse to keep from every episode, learned by heart in three short rounds.
• Questions the journey raises, and answers it returns to later.
• Your own reflections, and a printable book of your whole journey.

FOR FAMILIES, CLASSES AND CHURCHES
• Group mode: take turns reading Scripture aloud, decide together, show discussion questions to the room, and print a leader's guide.
• Gentle on young players: the crucifixion and other hard events are told truthfully and without gore.

ADVENT CALENDAR
• Twenty-five doors from December 1 to Christmas Day, each opening a reading, a thought, a carol and the memory it belongs to.

IN ENGLISH AND SPANISH
• Recorded voices throughout.
• Four public-domain Bibles: World English Bible, King James Version, American Standard Version and Reina-Valera 1909.

FREE, WITH NOTHING ELSE
No ads, no accounts, no purchases, no tracking. Everything is inside the app and plays offline.

Scripture, voices and music are inside the app. Try The Birth of Jesus first (timeline → ✦), the Advent calendar on the
title screen, and the shepherds' field, where you sweep your finger across the angels to make them sing.
