Privacy

The Way: An Eyewitness Account · © 2026 Jacob Cooper

The Way collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, your name in the story, your reports, your own words and your settings are saved only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

When the app uses the internet

The game, its Bibles and its recorded voices are inside the app and need no connection. The app reaches the internet only if you choose to download another Bible translation in Settings; then it asks a public Bible service for that text and nothing else.

Notifications

If you turn on the Advent reminders, your phone schedules them itself. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

The Way is suitable for families and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Questions

Write to the developer through the contact link on the App Store or Google Play page.

Privacidad

The Way no recoge nada sobre ti. No hay cuentas, ni publicidad, ni estadísticas, ni seguimiento.

Tu progreso, tu nombre en la historia, tus informes, tus propias palabras y tus ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo. Nunca se envían a ninguna parte. Al borrar la aplicación se borran.

El juego, sus Biblias y sus voces grabadas están dentro de la aplicación y no necesitan conexión. Solo usa internet si decides descargar otra traducción de la Biblia en Ajustes.

Si activas los recordatorios de Adviento, tu teléfono los programa por sí mismo. No se nos envía nada.
